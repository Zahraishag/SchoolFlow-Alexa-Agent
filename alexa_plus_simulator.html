Agent Workflow

1. Voice Request Administrator reports an absence through Alexa.
2. Candidate Validation Conflict, subject, stage, and workload rules are checked.
3. AI Recommendation The safest eligible candidate is presented.
4. Human Decision The administrator must explicitly approve or reject.
5. Safe Execution Prototype schedule state is updated only after approval.
AI recommends → deterministic rules validate → human approves → system executes safely.
